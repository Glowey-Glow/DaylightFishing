# Glowey's Fishing

**Fishing Mode for Daylight UI on WoW: Forever:** everything set up for fishing with one click, and put back as it was when you're done.

By Glowey. A standalone addon that **integrates with Daylight UI** when it's installed: its pages sit in Daylight UI's options window, in its look, and ride in its profiles (not part of Daylight UI itself: report its bugs here, not to Daylight UI). Without Daylight UI, its settings are in the Glowey's Plugins window (**/glowey**, or Options > AddOns).

## Turn on Fishing Mode
- click the **Fishing Mode** data text on your Daylight UI data bars;
- type `/fish`;
- use the **Fishing Mode** macro (make it with **Make a Fishing Mode Macro** in the settings and drag it to an action bar);
- or just put on a fishing pole: Fishing Mode turns on by itself, and off when you take the pole off.

## While it's on
- **Your best fishing pole** goes on, and the sound is set up for fishing (effects up, music and ambience down).
- **A fishing menu** under your character: your bait (click to put it on), the bait timer, the cast button and the weapons that go back on afterwards.
- **One key** casts, and hooks the fish once the bobber is out: Space by default, or any key or mouse button you like (**Set Fishing Key**).
- **The rest of the UI fades away** while you stand and fish, keeping the menu, minimap, chat and bags.
- **A catch counter** for the session (hover it for the totals, click it for the list).
- **Fishing skill:** the bar at the top of the menu shows your skill against your rank's cap. Click it to see where to learn your next rank, and click a place for a waypoint.

**Pulled into a fight?** Fishing Mode ends ("Combat Detected, Exiting Fishing Mode") and stays off. Your main hand and off hand (a one-hand and shield too) go back on at once, and your weapons always come back however Fishing Mode ends. **First start:** you pick your fishing key before the welcome (with Forever Fishing loaded, it tells you which key that one uses). With Mastercraft installed, Glowey's Fishing handles fights by default (asked once), and Mastercraft's "Hide the game's interface" waits while our UI fade is on, so the UI never stays faded.

Everything goes back as it was when Fishing Mode ends.
