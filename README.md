# Daylight Fishing

A plugin for DaylightUI on WoW: Forever: Fishing Mode, from a button on your data bars.

- **Fishing Mode** (the "Fishing Mode" data text, or `/dui fish`): puts on your best fishing pole, sets the sound up for fishing (effects up, music and ambience down, sound in the background), and turns on soft interact and auto loot. Everything goes back as it was when it ends, or when you take the pole off. Putting a pole on turns it on.
- **A macro for it:** "Make a Fishing Mode macro" in the settings gives you a "Fishing Mode" macro with the Fishing icon, ready to drop on an action bar.
- **Fishing Skill** on top of the menu: a bar to your rank's cap with a mark where the next rank can be learned, and "Maximum Fishing Trained" at Artisan. Click it for where to go next: the closest fishing trainers (your faction's and neutral ones), the Expert book's seller or the Artisan quest giver, each a waypoint on click.
- **One key** casts, and hooks the fish once the bobber is out.
- **A menu under your character** with your bait (click to put it on the pole), the bait timer and the cast button.
- **Catch counter** for the session: hover it for everything you caught, click it for a list fish by fish. A popup says "Fish Caught!" or "Fish Got Away" after each cast.
- **Quiet screen:** the UI fades away while you stand and fish, keeping the fishing menu, minimap, chat and bags.

Settings: DaylightUI options, Plugins, Fishing.
