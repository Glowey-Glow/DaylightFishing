# Daylight Fishing

**Fishing Mode for DaylightUI on WoW: Forever:** everything set up for fishing with one click, and put back as it was when you're done.

Requires **DaylightUI**.

---

## Turn on Fishing Mode
- click the **Fishing Mode** data text on your DaylightUI data bars;
- type `/dui fish`;
- use the **Fishing Mode** macro (make it with **Make a Fishing Mode macro** in the settings and drag it to an action bar);
- or just put on a fishing pole: Fishing Mode turns on by itself, and off when you take the pole off.

## While it's on
- **Your best fishing pole** goes on, and the sound is set up for fishing (effects up, music and ambience down).
- **A fishing menu** under your character: your bait (click to put it on), the bait timer and the cast button.
- **One key** casts, and hooks the fish once the bobber is out: Space to start, or any key or mouse button you like (**Set Fishing Key**).
- **The rest of the UI fades away** while you stand and fish, keeping the menu, minimap, chat and bags.
- **A catch counter** for the session (hover it for the totals, click it for the list).
- **Fishing skill:** the bar at the top of the menu shows your skill against your rank's cap. Click it to see where to learn your next rank, and click a place for a waypoint.

**Pulled into a fight?** Fishing Mode ends ("Combat Detected, Exiting Fishing Mode") and stays off. Your main hand goes back on at once, your off hand as the fight ends, and your weapons always come back however Fishing Mode ends. With Mastercraft installed, Daylight Fishing handles fights by default (asked once).

Everything goes back as it was when Fishing Mode ends.

---

## In your language
Daylight Fishing speaks German, French, Spanish, Russian, Korean and Simplified Chinese (machine drafts, corrections welcome; words the game already translates use the game's own wording), and finds trainers, places and keys on every game client.

---

## Settings
DaylightUI options > **Plugins > Fishing**. Window opacity follows DaylightUI's, or set your own there.
