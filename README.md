# Daylight Fishing

A plugin for DaylightUI on WoW: Forever that sets you up for fishing with one click: Fishing Mode.

## Requirements

- DaylightUI.

## Installing

Install it from CurseForge (or WowUp), or unzip it into `World of Warcraft\_forever_\Interface\AddOns`. Make sure it's ticked in the AddOns list on the character screen, then log in or `/reload`.

## How to use it

**Turn on Fishing Mode** in any of these ways:

- click the **Fishing Mode** data text on your DaylightUI data bars;
- type `/dui fish`;
- use the **Fishing Mode** macro (make it with **Make a Fishing Mode macro** in the settings and drag it to an action bar);
- just put on a fishing pole: Fishing Mode turns on by itself, and turns off when you take the pole off.

**While it's on:**

- your best fishing pole goes on, and the sound is set up for fishing (effects up, music and ambience down);
- a fishing menu appears under your character with your bait (click to put it on), the bait timer and the cast button;
- one key casts, and hooks the fish once the bobber is out;
- the rest of the UI fades away while you stand and fish, keeping the menu, minimap, chat and bags;
- a counter keeps your catches for the session (hover it for the totals, click it for the list).

Everything goes back as it was when Fishing Mode ends.

**Fishing skill:** the bar at the top of the menu shows your skill against your rank's cap. Click it to see where to learn your next rank; click a place there for a waypoint.

## Settings

Open the DaylightUI options and go to **Plugins → Fishing**.
